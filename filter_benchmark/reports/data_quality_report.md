## Timestamp integrity statement

Per-frame `t_us` comes from `esp_timer_get_time()` at the MCU when the sample is read. USB receipt time is not used anywhere in the benchmark. Duplicate `t_s` values arise when two sensors are read in the same lockstep tick (same physical instant), not from re-stamped data. Derivatives in later phases use these physical timestamps and measured (non-constant) intervals.
